### Key Territorial & 2024–2026 Reform Findings

1. **Territorial VAT Status Confirmations:**
* **Isle of Man (IM):** Part of the **UK VAT area** (under the Isle of Man–UK Common Purse Agreement). Sales to IM customers count toward your UK £90,000 domestic threshold; nothing extra applies below £90,000.
* **Monaco (MC):** Part of the **French / EU VAT area**. Selling to a non-VAT-registered Monaco food truck triggers immediate EU Non-Union OSS registration (nil threshold), whereas selling to a Monaco buyer with a valid `FR` VIES VAT number is subject to the B2B reverse charge.
* **Åland Islands (AX):** **Outside the EU VAT area** (Art. 6 of the EU VAT Directive), **but** administered by the Finnish Tax Administration (*Vero*) under the Finnish VAT Act, which applies Finnish 25.5% VAT to cross-border B2C/unregistered electronic services with a **nil threshold** for non-residents (and cannot be reported via EU OSS—requires direct Finnish registration unless the buyer is VAT-registered).
* **French Overseas Departments & Collectivities:** **Guadeloupe (GP), Martinique (MQ), and Réunion (RE)** are outside the EU VAT Directive area (so EU OSS does not cover them), *but* French domestic VAT (*Code général des impôts* Art. 259 B & 294) applies an 8.5% TVA rate to electronic services supplied to non-VAT-registered customers there, requiring direct French non-resident registration with a nil threshold. By contrast, **French Guiana (GF)** and **Mayotte (YT)** have **0% / no TVA** in force under Art. 294 CGI, and **Saint Barthélemy (BL)**, **Saint Martin (MF)**, and **Saint Pierre and Miquelon (PM)** have fiscal autonomy with **no non-resident SaaS VAT registration** regime.
* **Australian External Territories:** **Christmas Island (CX)**, **Cocos (Keeling) Islands (CC)**, and **Norfolk Island (NF)** are **excluded** from the Australian "indirect tax zone" under Sec. 195-1 of the *A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999*—Australian GST does **not** apply there.


2. **Major 2025–2026 Legislative Changes Caught in This Batch:**
* **South Africa (ZA):** Effective **1 April 2026**, SARS more than doubled the non-resident electronic services VAT registration threshold from ZAR 1,000,000 to **ZAR 2,300,000/yr** (~£100,000) and excluded pure B2B supplies to SA-registered vendors.
* **Mauritius (MU):** Effective **1 October 2026** (Finance Act 2026), Mauritius abolished the "nil threshold" and local tax representative requirement for foreign digital providers, introducing a **MUR 3,000,000/yr** (~£50,000) threshold and carving out VAT-registered B2B customers.
* **Rwanda (RW) & Morocco (MA):** Enacted new non-resident B2C/unregistered digital VAT regimes in **April 2026 (RW)** and **11 June 2026 (MA)**.
* **Mauritania (MR), Togo (TG), Chad (TD), Malawi (MW), Sierra Leone (SL), DR Congo (CD), and Grenada (GD):** All enacted or brought into force non-resident digital service VAT rules in **2026**.
