### Deep-Dive: United States — States Where B2B SaaS Is Taxable & Economic Nexus Thresholds (2026)

In the United States, there is no federal VAT. State sales tax is a **single-stage retail tax** on the end user. Because a food truck uses HatchGrab to process its orders and kitchen display (rather than reselling software licenses), **B2B SaaS is taxable in 22 states plus DC (and locally in 3 others)**.

#### 1. Sell Freely (Even to Unregistered Food Trucks — No Local Threshold Risk)
