## 2 · What now guarantees no horizontal scroll — named

**Four things, in the order they take effect:**

| # | Where | What | Why it is the guarantee |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `<img width={80} height={80}>` | 🔴 **HTML ATTRIBUTES, not just classes** | **The only defence that survives CSS not applying.** An unstyled `<img>` with these attributes is 80×80, not 3811px. **This directly targets the symptom you reported** |
| 2 | `<img className="… max-w-[80px] shrink-0">` | A hard ceiling plus no-shrink | The element cannot exceed 80px even if `w-20` were overridden |
| 3 | Row: `flex items-start gap-3 **w-full min-w-0**` | The row can never exceed its parent | `w-full` caps it at the card's content box; `min-w-0` lets it shrink if it ever becomes a flex item |
| 4 | Text column `flex-1 min-w-0`, **name row `min-w-0`**, name **`break-words`**, price row **`gap-2 min-w-0`** | The min-width chain | 🔴 **A nested flex container defaults to `min-width:auto` and refuses to shrink below its content.** `min-w-0` at **every** level plus `break-words` is what lets a long token wrap instead of pushing the row wide |

> 🔴 **I DID NOT USE `overflow-hidden` OR `overflow-x-clip`.** Either would guarantee "no scrollbar" by
> **clipping content off-screen**, which violates your harder requirement that nothing may be off-screen.
> **The min-width chain achieves it without hiding anything** — verified by counting elements whose
> `getBoundingClientRect().right` exceeds the viewport: **0 at every width tested.**
