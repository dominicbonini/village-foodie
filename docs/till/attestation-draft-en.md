LETTER - VAT - Model of the individual attestation on the use of secure till software or a secure till system

Parts 1 and 2 of this attestation must be shown to the tax administration on inspection. It has value only if its part 2 is duly completed and signed by the business that uses the software / system.

## I. Part 1: to be completed by the editor or integrator of the till software or system

I, the undersigned, [Full name], legal representative of the company HatchGrab Ltd, editor of the till software / system "HatchGrab, till module" (the till module of the HatchGrab software, reached through the website hatchgrab.com and through the HatchGrab apps for iOS and Android, which load the same site), attest that the till functions of this software/system (1), placed on the market from [DATE version 1.0.0 goes live], in its version no. 1.0.0, under licence number [the user business's HatchGrab truck id] (2), meet the conditions of inalterability, security, conservation and archiving of data for the tax administration's inspection set by article 286 I 3° bis of the French general tax code.

[I attest that the latest major version of this software or system is identified with the root: 1, and that minor versions developed after this major version are or will be identified by the following subdivisions of that root: 1.y.z. I undertake that HatchGrab Ltd will use these subdivisions only to identify later minor versions, never a major version. Major and minor versions of the software or system are as defined in III-A § 340 of BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30] (3)

The perimeter covered by this attestation is the following functions: (1) recording orders and their successive versions (at the hatch, online and offline), recording payments (charges, refunds, reversals of a charge), corrections by plus and minus entries, the chaining and sealing of records, the day, month and year (or accounting-year) closes with their grand totals, the conservation of data, the generation of archives and their verification.

The following functions are not covered by this attestation: (1) menus and products, marketing (posts, social networks, reviews, loyalty), scheduling and events, messaging, the e-invoicing / e-reporting file and business-to-business invoicing, and any third party's payment software or terminal.

Done at [CITY], on [DATE],

Signature of the legal representative of the editor of the till software or system:

[Full name], director, HatchGrab Ltd

(1) To be adapted and completed as the case may be;

(2) When a licence exists;

(3) An optional statement for the editor to make so that the tolerance in III-B § 380 of BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30 can apply.

Note: making a false attestation is a criminal offence punishable by three years' imprisonment and a €45,000 fine (French criminal code, art. 441-1). Using a false attestation carries the same penalties.
