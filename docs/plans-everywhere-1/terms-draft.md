- **Before:** the live English terms ("Last updated: 1 November 2026").
- **After:** the proposed wording, quoted word for word.

## 1 · The fee applies to every pre-order

**Section 4, "HatchGrab fees on online card orders"**

> **Before:** **HatchGrab fees on online card orders.** Depending on your plan, we charge a stated percentage of the value of online orders your customers pay for by card — on all of it, on the value above the amount your plan includes each month, or not at all. **We charge no fee on Pay at Hatch orders or on walk-up orders.**

> **After:** **HatchGrab fees on pre-orders.** Depending on your plan, we charge a stated percentage of the food value of every pre-order placed through HatchGrab, whether your customer pays online or at the truck: on all of it, on the value above the amount your plan includes each month, or not at all. The fee doesn't apply to tax, tips, service fees or surcharges, or to cancelled or rejected orders. If you refund a card payment, our fee goes down in proportion. **We charge no fee on walk-up orders.**

## 2 · Monthly invoicing where the fee can't be taken from the payment

**Section 4, "Paid in advance, and non-refundable"**

> **Before:** **Paid in advance, and non-refundable.** Monthly plan fees are billed one month in advance. **Fees already paid are non-refundable**, including if you cancel part-way through a month or stop using the service. This is the trade for there being no contract and no minimum term. Nothing here affects rights you have that cannot lawfully be excluded.

> **After:** **When we charge.** Monthly plan fees are charged in advance, on the 1st of each month. If you choose a paid plan when your free trial ends, we charge the rest of that month on the day it ends. If you upgrade, we charge the difference for the rest of the month straight away. A downgrade takes effect on the 1st of the next month. **Fees already paid are non-refundable**, including if you cancel part-way through a month or stop using the service. This is the trade for there being no contract and no minimum term. Nothing here affects rights you have that cannot lawfully be excluded.

**Section 4, new paragraph after "When we charge"**

> **After (new):** **How we collect our fee on pre-orders.** Where your country allows it, we take our fee from each online card payment when the order is paid. Everywhere else, including every country where your customers pay at the truck, we add up our fee on last month's pre-orders and charge it to your card on the 1st, together with your plan fee for the coming month. The pricing page for your country says which applies. If a monthly charge is below your country's minimum, we carry it over to the next 1st.
>
> **Your payment method.** Where we charge you monthly, you need a card on file (or Pix Automático in Brazil) when your free trial ends, and you authorise us to charge it automatically for the amounts above. Without one, online ordering pauses until you add one.

**Section 5, "We take only the platform fee agreed for your plan"**

> **Before:** **We take only the platform fee agreed for your plan**, deducted at the point of the transaction. Everything else is yours.

> **After:** **We take only the fee agreed for your plan**, deducted at the point of the transaction where your country allows it, and otherwise charged monthly as described in section 4. Everything else is yours.

## 3 · The 5-day pause

**Section 4, "If payment is not made"**

> **Before:** **If a payment fails or is not made, your account moves to the plan shown for your country on our pricing page, which in most countries has no monthly fee.** We do not lock you out.
>
> - If a payment is not made within **7 days** of its due date, your account moves to that plan. You keep your account, your menu, your orders and your data, and you can carry on taking orders and using the kitchen screen and dashboard. You lose any features that plan does not include, and that plan's fee on online card orders applies, if it has one.
> - Paying the outstanding amount restores your plan.
> - Moving plan does not entitle you to a refund of fees already paid, and you remain liable for amounts due up to that date.

> **After:** **If a payment fails, we retry it and email you.** You have the rest of that day and **5 more full days** to pay. If it is still unpaid after that, **online ordering pauses at midnight (your local time) until the amount is paid.**
>
> - Your account, your menu, your orders and your data stay as they are.
> - Paying the outstanding amount (from the Billing screen, or by updating your card) turns online ordering back on.
> - A pause does not entitle you to a refund of fees already paid, and you remain liable for amounts due.

The "⚠️ Plan for this" note after this section stays as it is.

**Section 4, "Price changes"**

> **Before:** **Price changes.** We may change our prices, fees or what a plan includes. We will give you at least 30 days' notice before a change affects you, and you may change plan or cancel before it takes effect.

> **After:** **Changes to prices and billing.** We may change our prices, our fees, what a plan includes, or how we collect our fee in your country. We will give you **at least 30 days' notice** before a change affects you, and you may change plan or cancel before it takes effect.
