- truck (the business) → food truck (class 9/10: food truck yako / food truck hii / food truck zako); with a name, keep {truck} free-standing and let agreement fall on another noun: passive "… itathibitishwa na {truck}", "kutoka {truck}", "kwa {truck}", "wasiliana na {truck}", "tukio la {truck}" — never a verb prefix that depends on {truck} ("{truck} itathibitisha") (part 01, order.willConfirm, order.notOpenBody, emailCustomer.payNote.heldBody, emailCustomer.rejection.body)
