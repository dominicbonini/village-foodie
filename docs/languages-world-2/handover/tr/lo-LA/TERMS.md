# TERMS — lo-LA (shared term sheet; append only)
- truck (the business) → Food Truck (Latin, spaces around it: "Food Truck ນີ້", "ໜ້າ Food Truck"); "the truck" with no name → Food Truck; {truck} stands alone ("{truck} ຈະຢືນຢັນ…", "ຕິດຕໍ່ {truck}"); "the vendor/them" in customer text → ທາງຮ້ານ ("ກະລຸນາສອບຖາມທາງຮ້ານ") (part 01, order.state.menuUnavailable, order.backToTruckPage, order.timeNotSetBody)
- Van (fleet vehicle) → ລົດ — never "Food Truck"; "Assign a truck to this event" (fleet sense) → "ກະລຸນາກຳນົດລົດໃຫ້ງານນີ້…"; "Choose which van’s kitchen screen" → "ເລືອກລົດທີ່ຕ້ອງການເປີດໜ້າຈໍຄົວ (KDS)"; "The van lost its internet connection" → "ລົດຂາດການເຊື່ອມຕໍ່ອິນເຕີເນັດ" (part 01, dashboard.settings.assignTruck, dashboard.kds.body, emailCustomer.offlineRejectReason)
- Settings sections (for later parts): "Truck settings" (the vans section) → "ການຕັ້ງຄ່າລົດ"; "Truck details" (the business) → "ຂໍ້ມູນ Food Truck"; "Your trucks" (vans list) → "ລົດຂອງທ່ານ"; + Add truck (van) → "+ ເພີ່ມລົດ" (part 01, decided with dashboard.settings.assignTruck)
