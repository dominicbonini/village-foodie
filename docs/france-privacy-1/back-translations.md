## am-ET

**Privacy Policy intro (A)**

**This policy applies wherever you are.** We follow the UK's data protection law; where the data protection law of the country you live in also applies, we follow that too.

**§4 Allergy and dietary notes (B)**

**Allergy and dietary notes.** We pass them to the food truck with your order; we do not use them for anything else. We remove them together with your name and contact details 12 months after the order (see section 7).

**§6 transfer sentence (C)**

When data leaves the UK, the transfer is covered by the UK adequacy regulations or by the International Data Transfer Agreement or the Addendum, and also, where your country's law requires it, by a safeguard that that law accepts.

**§8 Your rights (D)**

## 8. Your rights / Under UK data protection law, and also, where it applies, the law of the country you live in, you have the following rights: [7 bullets unchanged] / The law of the country you live in may give you these rights under another name, or additional rights. Those too are your rights. / **Making a request.** Send an email to privacy@hatchgrab.com. Before we take action, we will confirm that the request is genuine. You can ask another person to make the request for you; and we will not treat you differently for making a request. We will reply within one month, or earlier than that if your country's law requires it. If you ordered from a food truck, since it is the food truck that decides how your order details are used, we may pass your request to that food truck. If we reject a request, you can ask us to look at it again by responding to our answer. / **Deleting your account.** [old text unchanged] When a food truck owner's account is deleted, we also remove the names, contact details and notes of its customers on the orders we keep. / **Marketing emails.** We send marketing emails only where the law of the country you live in allows it; in some countries this means only with your consent. Each one states who it was sent by, contains our postal address and tells you how to stop receiving them. If you ask us to stop, we will stop free of charge and within 10 working days. Emails sent about an order or your account are not marketing. If we or a food truck offer order information by text message, you will receive them only if you agree; you can stop them at any time. / **If a problem occurs.** If a breach involving your personal data is likely to cause you serious harm, where the law requires it we will notify you and the regulatory body as quickly as possible; or we will help the food truck you ordered from to do this. / **Complaints.** First tell us by email at privacy@hatchgrab.com; we would appreciate it if you gave us the chance to put things right. You can also complain to the data protection regulatory body in the country you live in. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (the UK Information Commissioner's Office), on its website ico.org.uk or by telephone number 0303 123 1113.

**Country-specific terms intro (G)**

Our [Terms of Service] apply in each country where HatchGrab is used. Some countries have additional rules. This page sets them out one by one, country by country. / If a section below concerns you, it becomes part of our Terms of Service. If it says something different from them, this page applies for that country. / If your country is not listed, there is no additional rule: the Terms of Service apply as written. Our [Privacy Policy] is the same in all countries.
