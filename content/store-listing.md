## Full description — Play Store (4000 characters max)

```
HatchGrab is the ordering and kitchen system for street food and food truck operators.

Customers order from your own page. Orders arrive on your screen, in order, with the timings you set. No third-party marketplace, no commission on your sales.

TAKE ORDERS WITHOUT LOSING ANY

Orders come in from your customers' phones and land on your dashboard straight away. Add walk-up orders yourself in a few taps. Everything sits in one queue, so nothing gets missed at a busy hatch.

Push notifications tell you the moment an order arrives, even when the screen is off.

KEEP WORKING WHEN THE SIGNAL DROPS

Village pitches, festival fields and car parks are not known for their connection. HatchGrab keeps taking orders while you are offline and sends them when you are back. Your screen stays awake through a service so you are not unlocking a tablet with your hands full.

A KITCHEN SCREEN THAT SUITS YOUR SETUP

Run a kitchen display that shows what to cook and when. Choose the layout that fits how you work, set your own prep timings, and mark orders off as they go out. Connect a Bluetooth receipt printer if you would rather work from tickets.

Running more than one van? Each device can be set to its own van and its own starting screen.

YOUR MENU, YOUR PRICES, YOUR SCHEDULE

Build your menu with categories, options and add-ons. Set item prices, mark things as sold out mid-service, and record allergen information properly.

Add the events you are trading at, with dates, locations and times. Customers see where you will be next. Your ordering page updates itself.

SHARE IT ANYWHERE

Every truck gets its own ordering page and a QR code you can print for the hatch, a poster or a flyer. Share the link on social media. Customers order without downloading anything.

ANSWER CUSTOMERS WITHOUT STOPPING WORK

On paid plans, HatchGrab can reply to WhatsApp messages for you. Someone asking what is on the menu or where you will be on Saturday gets an accurate answer from your live data, while you carry on cooking.

BUILT FOR THE PEOPLE DOING THE COOKING

HatchGrab is made for operators, not for a marketplace. You keep your customers, your prices and your margin.

Free to try. UK based.
```

## Full description — App Store (4000 characters max)

Use the Play Store description above, with these differences:

- Apple does not permit references to other platforms or to pricing that competes with
  in-app purchase rules. Review before reuse.
- ⚠️ **Do not include a marketing URL to a site serving a different product.** A marketing
  link to something other than this app is worse than no link.

---

## What must NOT appear in either listing

- 🔴 **Messenger or Instagram auto-replies.** Both webhooks are stubs. The landing page
  currently claims these and that claim is a live problem, not a precedent to copy.
- 🔴 **Tap to pay / card payments through HatchGrab.** Coming soon in the product, not built.
- 🔴 **A website embed or an order page on the operator's own domain.** Not built.
- ⚠️ **Bluetooth receipt printing on Android** is built but has never been observed against a
  physical printer on Android. The copy above mentions printing generally; decide whether
  that is defensible for the Play listing before submitting.
- ⚠️ **Any claim about the number of trucks using the platform.** There are no onboarded
  customers.
