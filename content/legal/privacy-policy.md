# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 6 August 2026

This policy explains how HatchGrab Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses personal data across two connected services:

- **HatchGrab** (hatchgrab.com) — software used by food truck operators to take and manage orders.
- **Village Foodie** (villagefoodie.co.uk) — a public directory that helps people find food trucks trading near them.

It also covers the HatchGrab mobile and tablet apps for iOS and Android.

If you only came here because you ordered food from a truck, the section **"If you ordered from a food truck"** is the part that applies to you.

## 2. The two different roles we play

This distinction matters, so it is worth stating plainly.

**When you are a food truck operator using HatchGrab**, we are the controller of your account data. We decide what we collect and why, and you can exercise your rights directly against us.

**When you are a customer ordering food from a truck**, the truck is the controller of your order data. They decide what they collect and how they use it. We process that data on their behalf, under their instructions, as the provider of the software they use. If you want your order data deleted or corrected, the truck is the right first point of contact — though you can also contact us and we will help.

## 3. If you are a food truck operator

**What we collect**

- Account details: your name, email address, phone number, and the password you set (stored only as a cryptographic hash — we never see or store the password itself).
- Business details: trading name, business contact email and phone, business address, logo, menu, pricing, opening times and trading schedule.
- Usage data: which features you use, orders processed, and settings you change.
- Device data: for the mobile apps, a device identifier we generate, the device platform, and a push notification token if you enable notifications.
- Support correspondence: anything you send us by email or message.
- Payment and billing information, if and when paid subscriptions are activated. Card details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored on our systems.

**Why we use it, and our lawful basis**

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- |
| Creating and running your account | Performance of a contract |
| Providing the ordering, kitchen and scheduling features | Performance of a contract |
| Sending service emails (order alerts, account notices, password resets) | Performance of a contract |
| Sending push notifications you have enabled | Consent |
| Taking subscription payments | Performance of a contract |
| Keeping the service secure and preventing abuse | Legitimate interests |
| Improving the product and fixing faults | Legitimate interests |
| Marketing emails about our own services | Consent, or legitimate interests where you are an existing customer |
| Meeting accounting and legal obligations | Legal obligation |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, our interest is in operating and improving a reliable service. We have considered your rights and do not believe this processing overrides them. You can object at any time — see section 8.

**Biometric unlock.** If you turn on Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock in the app, the check happens entirely on your device and is handled by the operating system. We never receive, see or store biometric data. We only receive a yes-or-no result.

## 4. If you ordered from a food truck

When you place an order through a truck's ordering page, we collect and pass to that truck:

- Your name.
- Your email address, if you provide one, so the truck can send your confirmation and let you know your order is ready.
- Your phone number, if you provide one.
- Your order: the items, any notes or allergy information you add, the collection time, and the amount.

**We do not sell this data, use it for our own marketing, or share it with other trucks.** We use it only to deliver the ordering service to the truck you ordered from.

The truck decides how long to keep your details and how they contact you afterwards. Their own privacy practices apply to that. If you are not sure who the truck is, contact us at privacy@hatchgrab.com and we will point you to them.

**Allergen information.** Where a truck publishes allergen information, we display what the truck has confirmed. We do not verify it independently. Always tell the truck directly about a serious allergy.

## 5. If you visit our websites

We collect basic technical data automatically: IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and the site you arrived from. We use this to keep the sites working and secure, and to understand which pages are useful.

**Cookies.** We use cookies that are strictly necessary to make the sites work — signing you in, keeping your basket, and remembering your preferences. We do not use advertising, tracking or third-party analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may stop working if you block the necessary ones.

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Operator accounts:** for as long as the account is active, and for 12 months after it closes.
- **Order records containing customer details:** 12 months, after which the customer's name, email and phone are removed and only anonymised order data remains.
- **Anonymised order and sales data:** retained indefinitely, so trucks keep their trading history. This data cannot be linked back to an individual.
- **Accounting records:** six years from the end of the relevant financial year, as UK tax law requires.
- **Support correspondence:** 24 months.
- **Technical and security logs:** 90 days.

We delete or anonymise data once these periods end.

## 8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- ask what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data deleted, in certain circumstances;
- restrict how we use your data, in certain circumstances;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To exercise any of these, email privacy@hatchgrab.com. We will respond within one month.

**Deleting your account.** Operators can request account deletion from within the app or by emailing privacy@hatchgrab.com. We will delete your account and personal data, except where we are required to retain records for accounting or legal reasons.

**Complaints.** If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to address it first.
